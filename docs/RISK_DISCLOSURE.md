# Risk disclosure

* **You can lose some or all of the money you trade.** Crypto assets are highly volatile, trade
  24/7, and can gap through stop-loss levels. Exchanges can halt, get hacked or become insolvent.
* **Past performance, backtested or paper, does not guarantee future results.** Backtests are
  hypothetical: they assume the modelled fees, spread and slippage, and they are fitted to history
  that will not repeat. Paper trading cannot reproduce real market impact or real psychology.
* **Software fails.** Sentinel contains bugs. Network outages, exchange API changes, clock skew,
  and misconfiguration can all cause unintended orders or missed exits. The risk manager, kill
  switch and reconciliation reduce, but do not eliminate, these risks.
* **No advice.** Sentinel is a tool, not investment, tax or legal advice. You alone are responsible
  for how you configure and use it, and for complying with the laws and tax rules where you live.
* Only trade money you can afford to lose. Start on testnet, then paper, then LIVE with the
  smallest capital cap, and never use an API key with withdrawal permission.
